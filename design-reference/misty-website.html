How it unfolds

Each composition moves in three stages, so the scent you first notice is never quite the one you finish with.

Top

The first impression. Bright and brief, it lasts about fifteen minutes.

  • Bergamot
  • Pink pepper
  • Neroli

Heart

The character of the scent. Florals open and warm up on the skin.

  • Iris
  • Jasmine
  • Rose absolute

Base

What stays. Soft and resinous, it lingers into the next day.

  • Oud
  • Amber
  • Sandalwood

Why Misty

  1. Rare, traceable ingredients

    We buy absolutes directly from a small number of growers and publish where each one comes from.

  2. Made in small batches

    Perfumers compose each scent in limited runs, so every bottle is finished with the same care.

  3. Refillable by design

    Glass flacons refill from a pouch, which uses far less packaging and keeps your favourite bottle for years.

  4. Tested before you commit

    Every order includes two samples so you can wear a scent for a few days before choosing a size.

Questions

How long does delivery take?

Two to three working days across India. Shipping is free on orders above ₹999.

Can I return a fragrance?

Unopened bottles can be returned within seven days of delivery for a full refund. Opened bottles can be exchanged for a sample of another scent.

How long does a bottle last?

A 50 ml bottle used daily lasts roughly four months. Eau de Parfum typically holds for eight to ten hours on skin.

Are your fragrances authentic?

Every bottle is shipped directly from our atelier with a numbered seal. Scan the code on the box to check it.

Composed
in silence.

Misty began on a grey morning on the Normandy coast, when fog softened the horizon. We wanted to make a fragrance that feels like that moment, and then we kept at it for six years.

Every Misty fragrance starts with a single ingredient. We spend months with it, learn where it grows, and decide which scent it belongs to.

Compositions are built with independent perfumers in Grasse and rested for ninety days before bottling. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is added to make a scent louder.

We publish our ingredient sources, use refillable glass, and ship in recycled cartons. It's slower than most of the industry, and we think that's the point.

Traceable

Every absolute is sourced from a named grower, and the origin is printed on the box.

Patient

Ninety days of rest is the minimum. Good perfume cannot be hurried.

Restrained

We make fewer scents, and we remove any that don't meet the standard.

A good fragrance should feel like a memory you are only beginning to recall.
Elodie Marchand, founder and master perfumer
